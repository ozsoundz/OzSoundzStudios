About us

A family-rooted, musician-first recording studio built for the Australian independent music scene.

Our story

Who we
are

The Oz Soundz Group of businesses are sub-entities of Vestinex Pty Ltd (ABN 45 147 744 858), a wholly owned Australian private company that has been operating since 2010.

Founded by Jason “Plummo” Plumridge along with his family, The Oz Soundz Group serves the independent music industry through each of its business arms — working independently but in combination to support the music, audio, recording and performance industry in Australia and beyond.

Our PurposeMeet The Team

Whilst new in the market, The Oz Soundz Group has connection to the music & audio industry at the “grass roots local level” for more than four decades.

— Jason “Plummo” Plumridge, Founder
Our heritage

Our founder studied music at the Newcastle Conservatorium. His wife is a lover of all music styles. His eldest son is an accomplished keyboardist in the Sydney scene whose band launched an EP in 2024. His daughter is an accomplished singer, and his youngest son a trombonist and drummer. “Music is the lifeblood of our family.”
