Our purpose

Why do we exist? The soul behind everything we build.

Our vision

A studio that
believes in you

Our vision is to see Oz Soundz Studios become a recognised and trusted brand in the music, audio, film and recording industry in Australia — with a focus on supporting “grass roots” indie musicians on their career paths, and bringing new music to the community.

Music is the blood of life and the soul of the planet. The Oz Soundz Group was created by musicians for musicians — to support their musical endeavours, support all of our humanity, and bring peace, tranquillity and life to all through the medium of music.

Music is the blood of life and the soul of the planet.

— Oz Soundz Group mission
What drives us

Our core
values

Community First

We build everything around community. The Oz Soundz community is our reason for being — every product, service and decision serves it first.

Supporting Artists

We want to give back to the artist — through funding programs, events and recording sessions that get small artists into the industry.

Grassroots Roots

We don’t pretend to be what we’re not. We’re connected to the grassroots scene, and that shapes everything we do.

Accessible Pricing

Professional quality shouldn’t be reserved for major-label budgets. We’re committed to services real independent artists can afford.

Uncompromising Sound

Affordable doesn’t mean cheap. Our acoustic design, equipment and engineering standards will meet the needs of any professional artist.

Australian Owned

Proudly 100% Australian owned and operated — investing in Australian artists and keeping money in our own creative economy.

Stand with us

Support the Oz Soundz community and help us build something that lasts.
