About the studio

Where music
finds its voice

Oz Soundz Studios will provide a full range of audio recording, mixing and mastering services with our in-house engineers — and, by prior arrangement, use of the facility with your own pre-approved engineers.

Located in the Central Coast region of NSW and built with a single mission: give independent Australian artists a world-class space to create, at prices that don’t punish passion.

Music is the blood of life and the soul of the planet. The Oz Soundz Group was created by musicians for musicians to support their musical endeavours.
