Planning

Defining the vision, researching the market, running the numbers and choosing the site. Phase 01 · In progress

01

Define vision & goals

What the studio is for, who it serves and how it runs.

  • Purpose: music production, podcasting, post-production, voiceover
  • Target users: independent artists, commercial hire, in-house engineers
  • Model: owner-operated hybrid with approved external engineers
  • Values-led: community first, accessible pricing
02

Market research

Identifying competition and the best location; understanding demand, pricing and trends in audio production across the Central Coast, Hunter and Sydney regions.

  • Competitor mapping
  • Demand & pricing research
  • Audio production trends
  • Full SWOT analysis (in progress)
03

Budget & financial planning

Estimating the full cost of getting the doors open — and keeping them open.

  • Land / lease, construction, gear
  • Labour, permits, legal, insurance
  • Funding: self-funded, investors, loans, grants
  • ROI projections & operating forecasts
  • Monetisation strategy
04

Site selection

The criteria a site must meet before we commit. Buy vs lease is still under evaluation.

  • Commercial zoning
  • Sound restrictions & neighbours
  • Client accessibility & parking
  • Size for live room, control room, 2× iso, lounge
06

Business launch & operations

Everything that turns a building into a business.

  • Branding, website & marketing
  • Online booking system
  • Studio policies & session terms
  • Client management
07

Long-term maintenance & scaling

Keeping it running, sounding right and growing.

  • Maintenance schedule
  • Upgrade roadmap
  • Additional rooms
  • Financial sustainability & artist funding programs
