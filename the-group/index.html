The Oz Soundz Group

One ecosystem. Three arms. A single mission to support Australian music.

Brand journey

Building a
legacy

At The Oz Soundz Group our goal is to build a recognisable brand in the Australian music, audio, recording and live performance industry — recognised by grassroots and professional musicians alike, initially in Australia and ultimately around the world, with a primary focus on supporting the music industry at reasonable prices.

What makes us different is that we’re building our brand on “Community Support” — making sure you, the Oz Soundz community, have every level of support possible as you build your own careers in the industry. If you need support, The Oz Soundz Group is your first port of call.
