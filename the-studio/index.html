Design principles

How the rooms
are being designed

Golden ratios

Room dimensions based on acoustic “golden ratios” to keep standing waves under control.

Room within room

Floating floors, double walls and decoupled ceilings so the band doesn’t leak into the neighbours — or the booths.

Treatment

Bass traps, diffusers and absorbers tuned to an RT60 reverberation target set for each room.

Clean services

Isolated power, quiet HVAC with vibration isolation, and dimmable non-fluorescent lighting. No buzz.
