Studio services

Everything from the first take to the final master — with in-house engineers, or your own pre-approved engineer by arrangement.

01
Recording

Live & layered tracking

Full-band live takes or build-it-up overdubs. Acoustically treated live room, two isolation booths, and individual headphone mixes for every player.

  • Full band, solo, duo or ensemble
  • Drums, amps, vocals, acoustic instruments
  • Iso booths for separation — or play it live together
02
Post production

Mixing

Balance, depth and clarity that translates to earbuds, car stereos and club PAs. Attend the session or send stems in from elsewhere.

  • Tracked here or recorded elsewhere
  • Attended or unattended mixes
  • Revisions built into the process
03
Finalisation

Mastering

The final polish — loudness, tone and consistency across a release, ready for streaming platforms and physical formats.

  • Singles, EPs and albums
  • Streaming-ready deliverables
  • Consistent sequencing across a record
04
Spoken word

Podcast & voiceover

Treated rooms that make voices sound expensive. For podcasters, voice actors, audiobook narrators and content creators.

  • Multi-host podcast recording
  • Voiceover & narration
  • Edit and mix for release
05
By arrangement

Facility hire

Bring your own pre-approved engineer and use the rooms. Ideal for producers and engineers who need a proper space to work.

  • Pre-approval process for external engineers
  • House tech support on hand
06
Planned

Artist support programs

We plan to build funding programs, events and recording opportunities that help emerging artists get their music made and heard.

  • Details announced as they take shape
  • Register to hear first
